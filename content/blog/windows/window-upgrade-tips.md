Old, drafty windows can silently waste energy and make your home uncomfortable.  

### Signs You Need New Windows
- Condensation between glass panes  
- Drafts even when closed  
- Difficult operation or broken locks  

### Choosing the Right Upgrade
1. **Vinyl Windows** – affordable, low maintenance  
2. **Fiberglass Windows** – durable, strong, and energy-efficient  
3. **Wood-Clad Windows** – premium aesthetics with insulated cores  

> For most homes, vinyl or fiberglass windows offer the best long-term value.
