Deck design in 2025 is all about **creating outdoor spaces that feel like an extension of your home**.

### Top Deck Trends:
1. **Multi-level layouts** — split levels for dining and lounging  
2. **Composite materials** — modern, durable, and easy to maintain  
3. **Built-in seating** — benches and planters for smart use of space  

> Pro tip: Add subtle LED lighting along railings to make evenings more inviting.
