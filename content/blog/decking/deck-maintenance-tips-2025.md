A well-maintained deck can easily last 20+ years — and only needs consistent care throughout the seasons.  
Here’s a simple year-round maintenance guide to protect your outdoor investment and keep it looking new.

---

### **🌷 Spring**
- Wash away dirt, pollen, and winter grime  
- Inspect for loose boards or nails  
- Apply a light sealant if needed  

---

### **☀️ Summer**
- Sweep regularly to prevent debris buildup  
- Move furniture occasionally to avoid fading spots  
- Re-stain or reseal if color looks dull  

---

### **🍂 Fall**
- Clean gutters and sweep off leaves  
- Check for water pooling near the base of your deck  
- Tighten railings and inspect steps before winter  

---

### **❄️ Winter**
- Avoid salt-based ice melt on wood  
- Shovel snow along the grain to prevent scratches  
- Keep the deck free of standing water or ice  

---

### **Final Tip**
Regular maintenance means you’ll save money long-term and prevent costly structural repairs.  
A clean, sealed deck not only looks beautiful but also protects your home’s value.
