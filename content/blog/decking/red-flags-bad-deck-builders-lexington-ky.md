## 7 Red Flags 🚩 of Bad Deck Builders in Lexington, KY

Building a new **deck** should be exciting — not stressful. Unfortunately, some contractors cut corners, disappear mid-project, or leave you with unsafe results.  
As deck professionals with decades of experience in **Lexington, KY**, we’ve seen how small red flags early on can save homeowners thousands later.

Here are **7 red flags** every homeowner should recognize before hiring a deck contractor.

---

##  No License, Insurance, or References

A legitimate deck builder always provides proof of licensing and insurance.  
If someone avoids these questions, that’s your first red flag.  

Ask for:
- General liability and workers’ comp certificates  
- Local business license (Lexington-Fayette County)  
- A few recent client references or photos  

> **Tip:** A professional like [Yellowstone Renovation’s deck team](/decking/) will proudly show credentials upfront.

---

##  Vague or Verbal Estimates Only

A trustworthy contractor gives **written estimates** that detail labor, materials, and timelines.  
If they say “we’ll figure it out later” or “it depends on what happens,” you’re taking a financial gamble.  

Always ask for:
- Total project cost  
- Type of decking materials (wood, composite, or PVC)  
- Start and completion dates  

---

##  Unrealistically Low Prices

If a quote seems too good to be true — it usually is.  
Cheap bids often mean sub-grade lumber, underpaid crews, or skipped permits.  

Remember: good materials like **composite decking** aren’t cheap, but they last decades with minimal upkeep.  
Cutting costs now often means paying more for repairs later.

---

##  No Building Permit or Code Knowledge

In Lexington, decks over 30 inches high or attached to a home **require a permit**.  
Bad contractors either don’t know this — or worse, they ignore it.  

> **Avoid anyone who says:**  
> “You don’t need a permit for that.”  

Working with professionals ensures your deck meets code, passes inspection, and stays safe long-term.

---

##  Poor Communication or Missed Appointments

Good builders respect your time.  
If a contractor can’t return calls, shows up late, or constantly reschedules — expect the same once construction starts.  

Reliable deck companies keep you updated at every stage, from material delivery to final inspection.

---

##  No Contract or Change-Order Policy

Contracts protect both sides. If there’s no agreement in writing, you’re vulnerable to delays, surprise fees, or unfinished work.  

A professional builder includes:
- Scope of work  
- Payment schedule  
- Warranty terms  
- Procedure for change orders  

> **Example:** At Yellowstone Renovation, every **decking project** has clear documentation before we start — no surprises.

---

##  Lack of Local Experience

Building decks in Kentucky’s climate requires knowing soil types, frost depth, and humidity challenges.  
Out-of-town or new contractors may not understand these factors.  

Choose a team that’s local, established, and familiar with Lexington permitting and inspection processes.

A solid deck starts with a solid contractor.  
When hiring, trust your instincts — if something feels off, it probably is.
