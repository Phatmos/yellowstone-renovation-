Selecting the ideal siding depends on three key factors — **climate, budget, and style**.  

### 1. Vinyl Siding
Affordable, versatile, and available in countless colors. Perfect for moderate climates.

### 2. Fiber Cement Siding
Resistant to fire, insects, and rot. Requires little maintenance and mimics real wood textures.

### 3. Wood Siding
Genuine look and feel, but needs regular sealing to protect against moisture.

> Tip: Always factor in long-term maintenance costs before making a final choice.
