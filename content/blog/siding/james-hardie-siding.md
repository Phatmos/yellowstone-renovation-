James Hardie siding has become the gold standard for homeowners who want timeless style and unbeatable durability.  
Its fiber cement composition offers long-term resistance to fire, moisture, and rot.  

### Why Choose James Hardie
- Superior resistance to harsh weather  
- Fade-resistant finishes  
- 30-year warranty  

### Design Options
Available in dozens of colors and textures, Hardie siding matches both modern and traditional homes.

> Pro tip: Combine horizontal planks with board-and-batten for a custom aesthetic.
