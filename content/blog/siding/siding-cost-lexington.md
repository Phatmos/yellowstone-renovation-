## Average Siding Replacement Costs in Lexington, KY

In 2025, the **average cost to replace siding in Lexington** typically ranges from **$3.50 to $8.50 per square foot**, depending on the material, condition of your home, and complexity of installation.  

That means a typical 2,000-square-foot home may cost anywhere between **$10,000 and $20,000** for a full siding replacement project.

| Siding Material | Average Installed Cost (per sq. ft.) | Lifespan | Maintenance Level |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------|-----------|------------------|
| Vinyl | $3.50 – $6.50 | 20–30 years | Low |
| Fiber-Cement (James Hardie) | $6.50 – $10.00 | 30–50 years | Very Low |
| Engineered Wood | $5.50 – $8.50 | 25–40 years | Moderate |

> **Local Insight:** In Lexington, vinyl and fiber-cement are by far the two most common choices — affordable, low-maintenance, and capable of handling Kentucky’s humid summers and cool winters.

## Factors That Affect the Cost of Siding Replacement

Every home and project is different, but these are the main variables that influence siding replacement pricing in Lexington.

### 1. Type of Material
Material selection is the biggest cost driver.  
- **Vinyl** is the most affordable and easiest to maintain.  
- **Fiber-cement** (like James Hardie) provides durability, fire resistance, and great curb appeal.  
- **Wood or metal siding** gives a high-end look but needs more upkeep or costs more to install.

### 2. Home Size and Design
The **larger or more complex the home**, the higher the labor and material cost.  
Multi-story homes, gables, dormers, or decorative trim increase surface area and installation time.

### 3. Removal of Old Siding
If your old siding needs to be removed, expect to pay **$1,000–$3,000** for removal and disposal.  
Some older homes also require sheathing repair or insulation upgrades underneath.

### 4. Trim and Accessory Details
Decorative trim, fascia boards, soffits, and corner posts all add to labor.  
Upgrading to insulated siding or decorative textures will also raise costs slightly but improve efficiency.

### 5. Labor and Local Market Conditions
In Lexington, skilled siding installers typically charge **$40–$70 per hour**, depending on experience and project size.  
Always confirm that your contractor is licensed, insured, and familiar with **Kentucky building codes**.

## Vinyl vs Fiber-Cement — What’s Best for Kentucky?

Here in Central Kentucky, both vinyl and fiber-cement perform well — but each has distinct strengths.

| Feature | Vinyl Siding | Fiber-Cement (James Hardie) |
|----------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Cost | Lower upfront | Higher upfront |
| Maintenance | Minimal (no painting) | Needs repainting every 10–15 years |
| Durability | Good | Excellent |
| Moisture Resistance | High | Very High |
| Insulation | Moderate (optional foam backing) | High thermal stability |
| Appearance | Many colors & textures | Premium look, wood-like finish |

If your goal is long-term durability and improved home value, fiber-cement is worth the investment.  
If you’re keeping to a tighter budget, modern vinyl siding options still look sharp and hold up surprisingly well in Kentucky’s humid climate.

## What Does the Process Involve?

A full siding replacement typically includes these steps:

1. **Inspection & Measurement** – Assess existing siding condition and measure wall surface area.  
2. **Removal of Old Siding** – Carefully detach existing material and inspect for damage underneath.  
3. **Wall Prep & Repairs** – Replace rotted sheathing or wrap with new moisture barrier.  
4. **Installation of New Siding** – Panels, trim, and accessories installed with proper flashing.  
5. **Painting/Finishing (if applicable)** – Fiber-cement and wood siding are often painted after installation.  
6. **Cleanup & Final Inspection** – Debris removal and final walkthrough to ensure quality workmanship.

## Why Homeowners Replace Siding

Replacing siding isn’t just about curb appeal — it’s about **protecting your home**.  
If you notice these issues, it’s time to call a professional:

- Cracked, warped, or missing panels  
- Soft or rotted boards  
- Frequent painting or color fading  
- Rising energy bills due to leaks or poor insulation  
- Mold or mildew growth around seams  

Fresh siding improves energy efficiency, boosts resale value, and dramatically enhances the appearance of your property.

## Maintenance Tips After Replacement

To get the most out of your new siding:

- Wash it gently once or twice a year to remove dust and mildew.  
- Keep landscaping trimmed at least six inches away from the walls.  
- Avoid high-pressure washers — use soft brushes and mild detergent.  
- Check caulking around windows and doors annually.  
- Inspect for loose panels after storms.

These small steps can add years to your siding’s lifespan.
