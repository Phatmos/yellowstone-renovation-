      FAQ

      Frequently asked questions

      Still have a question? Call or text us at (859) 765-7267.

      What happens at the in-home deck consultation?

      We meet at your home, measure the space and talk through layout, railing, stairs and materials, and answer every question. Then you get a clear estimate and a 3D design of your deck.

      How long does a deck build take?

      It depends on size, materials and permits. You get a realistic schedule in writing before we start, and your project manager keeps you updated the whole way.

      Do you handle permits and code compliance?

      Yes. Your project manager coordinates permits and inspections, and we build footings, framing, stairs and railing to code.

      Can you build composite or wood decks?

      Both. We build composite decks from brands like Trex and TimberTech, plus pressure-treated and cedar decks. We'll show you samples and the pros and cons of each.

      Do you offer repairs or full replacements?

      Yes. We repair, resurface and fully replace decks. During the visit we check the footings and framing and tell you honestly which option makes sense.

      Do you offer financing?

      Ask us during your visit and we'll walk you through the current financing options for your project.
